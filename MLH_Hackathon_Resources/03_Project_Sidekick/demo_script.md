# Sidekick — 90-second demo outline

1. **Problem (10s):** Beginners often get stuck, and asking for a complete answer can prevent learning.
2. **Show the floating control (10s):** Sidekick stays available but does not inspect the screen until the user asks.
3. **Trigger help (15s):** Click the button and approve/share a screenshot of a coding problem.
4. **Show the hint ladder (25s):** First a guiding question, then a more specific hint, then a deeper explanation.
5. **Show the learning loop (15s):** Make a change and click **Check again** for feedback on the updated screen.
6. **Gemini + privacy (10s):** Explain that Gemini interprets the user-approved screenshot, while capture is user-controlled and screenshots are not saved by default.
7. **Close (5s):** “Your work. Your thinking. Your Sidekick.”
